Grammarly is an AI-powered writing and editing assistant that helps users produce clearer, more correct, and audience-appropriate text. Beyond spelling and grammar checks, it offers suggestions for clarity, concision, tone, and rewriting.
Main features
Spelling and grammar
Detects spelling, punctuation, grammar, and commonly confused-word issues, then suggests corrections.
Clarity and concision
Identifies unclear or unnecessarily complex wording and recommends revisions that make writing easier to understand.

Grammarly is a writing assistant, not a substitute for subject-matter review, peer review, or verification of factual claims and references. Rewriting suggestions can alter nuance or specialized terminology, so authors should review every meaningful change. For academic writing, preserve the intended meaning, technical terms, and citations, and follow the relevant university, publisher, or journal policies on AI-assisted writing. Advanced capabilities may depend on the subscription plan and the language being used.
