LeapSpace is an AI-assisted research workspace from Elsevier. It is designed to help researchers discover, review, compare, and evaluate scholarly information while keeping human judgment and verification central to the research process.
Main features
Advanced literature search
Researchers can ask complex questions in natural language, refine queries through follow-up questions, and explore new angles of a research topic across scholarly content.
Deep Research
Generates structured research reports that can surface emerging patterns, contradictions, and evidence gaps. Results include references so that users can inspect the supporting literature.
Reading Assistant
Lets users ask questions about a specific article, book chapter, or supported PDF. It can summarize sections, identify key points, and connect answers to relevant passages in the document.
Compare Tables
Extracts and organizes information from multiple studies into comparison tables. Depending on the workflow, fields can include research aims, materials, methods, results, and conclusions. Tables may be exported as CSV.
Trust and transparency
Features such as Trust Cards and Claim Radar help users inspect how claims relate to cited sources and the wider research literature. They support verification rather than replacing it.
Find researchers and funding
LeapSpace can help identify researchers and potential collaborators in a subject area, as well as funding opportunities relevant to a research topic.
Writing Coach
The Writing Coach helps researchers challenge and strengthen their drafts by checking claims against relevant scholarly literature, considering the reasoning behind an argument, and surfacing potential evidence gaps. Suggested changes remain under the author's control.
File upload
Users can upload research files and ask questions about their contents, including questions that connect the uploaded material with the wider scholarly literature. File limits and availability may depend on the subscription or institutional configuration.
Academic use cases

- Exploratory literature discovery
- Extracting and comparing methods and findings across studies
- Checking whether scholarly sources support a claim
- Identifying possible research gaps and follow-up questions
- Finding researchers, collaborators, and funding opportunities
- Reviewing arguments and improving research drafts
  Limitations and responsible use
  LeapSpace is a research assistant, not a replacement for systematic searching in specialist databases, critical appraisal, or manual source verification. Its content is curated and should not be assumed to represent the entirety of scholarly literature. AI-generated answers may be incomplete or inaccurate, so claims, quotations, and references should be checked against the original publications. Feature availability and usage limits can vary by plan and institutional configuration.
