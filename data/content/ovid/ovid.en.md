## Overview

**Ovid** is a specialized medical research and information retrieval platform provided by Wolters Kluwer. It is designed to support healthcare professionals, researchers, librarians, and students in discovering and accessing scientific literature, medical databases, journals, books, and evidence-based resources.

Unlike PubMed or Embase, Ovid is not a standalone bibliographic database. It is an integrated research platform that provides access to a wide range of specialized databases and scholarly resources through a unified search interface.

The platform provides access to more than 100 research databases and information resources covering medicine, nursing, pharmacology, psychology, behavioral sciences, and other healthcare-related disciplines.

Availability of individual databases depends on institutional subscriptions and licensing agreements.

## Major Databases Available on Ovid

Some of the major databases and resources accessible through the Ovid platform include:

- **Ovid MEDLINE:** A major bibliographic database covering biomedical literature, medicine, nursing, and health sciences. It uses the Medical Subject Headings (MeSH) controlled vocabulary.
- **Embase:** A comprehensive biomedical and pharmacological database with extensive coverage of drug research, clinical studies, journal articles, and conference abstracts. It uses the Emtree controlled vocabulary.
- **APA PsycINFO:** A specialized database covering psychology, behavioral sciences, mental health, and related disciplines.
- **Ovid Emcare:** A database focused on nursing and allied health literature.
- **Cochrane Resources:** Access to resources such as the Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews and the Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials, depending on institutional subscriptions.
- **JBI EBP Database:** Resources supporting evidence-based healthcare practice.

Availability of individual databases depends on institutional subscriptions and licensing agreements.

## Key Features and Capabilities

### 1. Advanced Search

Ovid provides advanced search capabilities for developing structured and reproducible literature search strategies.

Researchers can combine Boolean operators, field-specific searches, search limits, and subject headings to retrieve relevant scientific records.

### 2. Controlled Vocabulary Searching

Ovid supports the controlled vocabularies associated with individual databases, including:

- MeSH for MEDLINE
- Emtree for Embase
- APA Thesaurus for PsycINFO

These thesauri help researchers identify standardized subject headings and improve the precision and comprehensiveness of their searches.

### 3. Multi-Database Searching

One of Ovid's major advantages is the ability to search multiple databases simultaneously through a unified interface.

Researchers can execute search strategies across multiple information sources without having to use entirely separate interfaces.

This functionality is particularly useful for systematic reviews and other comprehensive evidence synthesis projects.

### 4. Search History Management

Ovid provides a search history interface that allows users to review, combine, and manage individual search sets.

Researchers can build complex search strategies incrementally using Boolean operators such as AND, OR, and NOT.

Search histories can also be saved and reused, supporting transparent and reproducible research workflows.

### 5. Duplicate Detection and Removal

When searching multiple bibliographic databases, the same publication may appear in more than one database.

Ovid provides deduplication tools to help identify and remove duplicate records from search results.

This can reduce manual screening workload during systematic reviews.

### 6. Search Limits and Filters

Researchers can refine their search results using various database-specific limits, including:

- Publication year
- Language
- Publication type
- Study type
- Indexing status
- Population characteristics

Available filters vary depending on the selected database.

### 7. Search Saving and Alerts

Ovid supports saving search strategies, reusing previous searches, and sharing search histories.

Its AutoAlert (SDI) functionality can notify users about newly available records matching their saved search strategies.

## Research Applications

Ovid is particularly useful for:

- Systematic reviews
- Scoping reviews
- Comprehensive biomedical literature searches
- Evidence-based practice research
- Pharmaceutical and pharmacological research
- Nursing and allied health research
- Clinical and healthcare research
- Developing and documenting reproducible search strategies

## Access and Availability

Ovid is a commercial platform. Access to many of its databases and resources requires an institutional or organizational subscription.

Universities, hospitals, and research institutions may provide access through:

- Institutional networks
- University VPN services
- Institutional authentication systems
- Authorized remote access services

Having an Ovid account does not necessarily provide unrestricted access to all databases hosted on the platform.
